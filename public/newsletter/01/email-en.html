📡  Radar · 4 signals of the month
20%
ChatGPT now “weighs” a fifth of Google
OpenAI says ChatGPT search volume equals 20% of Google’s. Caveat: it only counts requests that trigger a web search and doesn’t say where the Google figure comes from.
plaiground take: Google isn’t dead, but there’s a second shop window now. And this one has no ten blue links: it has one answer.
Source: MarketingProfs
✓
Your brand can now have a “profile” in ChatGPT
OpenAI opened a beta hub where companies can claim and verify their ChatGPT presence (via phone or DNS record), see mentions, clicks and conversation topics, and test “Business Agents” that answer customers.
Action: claim it before anyone else and see which topics cite you. It’s the Google Business Profile of the agent era.
Source: MarketingProfs
24%
Everyone’s investing in AI… almost no one can prove it
Peec AI survey of 700 marketing decision-makers (US, UK, Germany): 89% raised AI search spend, but only 24% can show what it returns. Vendor-sourced data, so take it with a pinch of salt.
plaiground take: measurement is the competitive edge. Whoever has the dashboard gets the conversation with the CFO.
Source: Agile Brand Guide
11%
Google’s AI makes things up too
In a study of 55,393 searches, AI Overviews appeared on 13.7% (64.7% for questions). 11% of 98,020 claims had no support in the cited sources, and nearly 30% of cited sites weren’t on the first page of results.
Action: watch what AI says about your brand, not just where you rank. You can be cited without being in the top 10… and have something false said about you.
Source: MarketingProfs

🧪  Experiment of the month
What people search isn’t what it earns
I crossed two columns of the Franchise Brand Barometer (41 franchises): 12-month Google interest (Star Wars = 100) and each franchise’s total revenue. Minecraft generates 7.8× Star Wars’ interest while earning over 5× less. And Marvel, with 38 films and $44bn, gets half the searches of Star Wars.
My hypothesis: conversation isn’t revenue, but it’s what AI models read (and repeat). A brand that sells but that nobody talks about risks being invisible in the answer.
